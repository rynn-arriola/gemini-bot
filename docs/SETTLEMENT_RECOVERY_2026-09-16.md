# Gemini settlement recovery — September 16, 2026

At exactly 17:00 ET, Gemini published a lifecycle change for the held contract. The runtime treated any held-inventory lifecycle event as global fatal, canceled the unrelated resting cohort, and then could not force an exact exit because the affected book had no buyer. Later authenticated account truth showed the contract settled and the venue position closed. This exposed a second defect: the account reducer recognized `settlement_payout` only as generic activity and did not advance durable expected position/cash or realized P&L, so restart reconciliation remained stale.

The repair adapts the local Polymarket bot's market-local settlement-wait architecture without copying venue semantics. On Gemini, the affected symbol is quarantined with its original deadline and exposure while unrelated fully reconciled markets continue. Primary live proof is Gemini's authenticated terminal `positionReport` containing position zero and `settlement_payout`. Missed-event/restart recovery uses a bounded `eventTicker` read from `/v1/prediction-markets/positions/settled`, stable current position/order brackets, and one exact match on instrument symbol, outcome and signed quantity.

Settlement is durably distinct from a trade or non-trading reward. The immutable settlement row removes the expected position, advances expected cash by exact payout, clears the position deadline and closes its recorded fill basis for six-hour realized P&L. A simultaneous liquidity reward, maker rebate or transfer is still reconciled separately from its own authenticated history. Duplicate identical settlement evidence cannot double-credit cash. Missing/changed quantity or payout, truncated history, unstable account brackets, foreign positions or other account-wide ambiguity still stop new risk.

The owner also approved 20 contracts per outcome leg. The 30 pending-order limit, 15 two-sided-market ceiling, $300 gross, $90 per-market, $40 stressed-exit admission filter, five-minute exits and single $30 six-hour HARD are unchanged. A settled position consumes zero pending-order slots.
