# Safety invariants

Violation of an invariant blocks new risk and requires reconciliation or a terminal halt.

1. Production begins paused. Process startup alone can never submit an order.
2. Live activation requires accepted terms, fresh balances/positions/open orders, live public and private streams, a synchronized book, complete risk configuration, exact live acknowledgement, and an unexpired one-use version-2 owner permit bound to policy, Python build, account fingerprint and exact symbols. Run expiry ends quoting.
3. One Windows process owns all Gemini mutations. A second process fails closed.
4. Every mutation has a recorded intent before network send. An ambiguous order submission is never blindly retried.
5. Initial quotes are maker-only (`LIMIT` + `MOC`) and must satisfy current contract tick, increment, quantity, price, market-state, reward, effective-time, and expiry rules. Sports entry stops and any position becomes due for exact exit 20 minutes before the published game start even if in-play trading remains open. Crypto interval markets retain the separate five-minute pre-expiry cutoff.
6. Pending orders never exceed 30. Filled positions are excluded from that count but remain inside separate position and capital gates.
7. A first fill immediately removes permission to add on the same directional exposure and prioritizes cancellation of the opposite/risk-adding leg.
8. Exact authenticated position quantity—not intended or locally inferred quantity—controls reduction.
8a. A close is exact and outcome-correct: SELL YES for a positive YES position, SELL NO for a negative NO position. Full haircut-adjusted depth must cover it; a terminal IOC status alone never proves flat.
8b. A terminal IOC with a nonzero position may retry only after REST and authenticated WebSocket agree on the exact same-sign, non-increasing residual. Each retry uses fresh depth; the entire sequence is limited to three attempts and 30 seconds. Rejection or ambiguity never retries.
9. A differential-depth gap, crossed local book, stale shared market-data stream, contract closure, stale account state, or unknown ownership removes permission to quote. A quiet symbol may retain its last sequenced book while the single shared WebSocket has recent verified traffic; a one-sided book cannot enter the two-sided quote strategy.
10. REST cannot silently replace WebSocket streaming. It is bounded reconciliation and metadata transport.
11. Server throttles and `Retry-After` are honored. Safety priority never bypasses a server cooldown, and extra keys/connections are not used to evade limits.
12. A timed/expiry exit cancels risk-adding orders and confirms terminal state before sending an exact reducer.
13. The inventory deadline persists across restart and cannot be reset by a reconnect or process restart.
14. The single $30 six-hour Eastern stop uses completed matched bot fill lifecycles, including recorded fees. Open inventory, unrealized marks, rewards, deposits, withdrawals and unrelated cash changes do not enter realized trading P&L. Incomplete fill classification blocks new risk. No other monetary stop authority exists.
15. Six-hour Eastern realized-loss high-water persists across restart. Pending entries remain fixed at 30 below HARD and become zero at HARD; a restart, reward, or cash transfer cannot forgive a reached stop. The 300-second inventory and pre-expiry exits act on time, not on another loss threshold.
16. Shared reward budgets are counted once by pool ID, while uptime is isolated by event ticker. An unreported reward start uses a conservative Eastern-day/effective-time bound and is calibrated later against authenticated `total_snapshots`. Ranking v2 reward/share/cost/net values are conservative proxies and are never presented as guaranteed payout. Non-positive net and sub-$1 estimated rewards cannot enter, even under an uptime-only override.
17. Secrets, signatures, nonces, signed payloads, and sensitive account state are redacted from every output.
18. A prepared process never quotes without approval. Versions 2–4 remain one-use. Versions 5–6 permit at most two same-supervisor restarts before expiry, only after the prior runner durably proves flat; every restart repeats full reconciliation. Cross-session reuse, nonflat/ambiguous state, policy/build change, expiry, or exhausted authority requires fresh owner approval. Permits remain limited to 24 hours.
19. The uncertain daily-uptime feasibility proxy may be ignored only by explicit activation. Bounded commissioning requires exact owner-reviewed prices. Continuous use additionally requires a permit with a timezone-aware `uptime_override_until` no later than permit expiry. Version 4 can authorize dynamic selection only from Gemini's reconciled reward catalog; pool and pending-order caps still apply. The candidate remains ineligible in reward reports and the exception cannot bypass market, price, size, exposure, ownership, stream, permit, cancellation, fill, exit, or reconciliation gates. Versions 3–5 drain at override expiry. Owner-approved version 6 instead ends the exception exactly at its deadline and continues under normal eligibility until permit expiry, which always drains. New-day entries require freshly computed day-specific ranking; legacy behavior is unchanged.
20. A manual close is adopted only through explicit operator reconciliation after fresh REST/WebSocket proof of zero positions and active orders plus terminal bot intents. It clears stale deadlines but never fabricates fills or resets the current risk-window high-water.
21. The Windows supervisor never creates live authority. It can resume only within the exact version-5/6 build/policy/account/session bounds after durable flat proof. At most two restarts are allowed; three fast failures, any non-recoverable exit, or failed final reconciliation enters operator attention.
22. Every runtime/policy change includes an explicit 50%-snapshot impact review. Sticky uptime is preserved when safe, but never overrides exact exits, exposure limits, venue validity, ownership, freshness or reconciliation.
23. A public WebSocket depth move cancels a working entry pair when either leg is now closer than two ticks behind the corresponding touch. The replacement path remains serialized and requires terminal cancellation; movement farther from touch does not trigger fast cancellation churn.
24. The final maker send validates price and size against the current two-tick target after pacing. A stale unsent quote cannot be transmitted or silently edited inside its recorded intent; cancel its sibling and rerank.
25. Urgent entry cancellations run independently of placement/exit waits. Concurrent callers share one cancellation mutation per order; confirmation and unknown-outcome protections remain mandatory. Cancel requests take priority at the next available transport slot without bypassing venue cooldowns.
26. A manual-flat proof cannot classify missing closing proceeds or fees as zero loss. Unmatched fill basis at a manual-flat audit blocks the affected risk window. Authenticated closing evidence plus reconciliation may restore classification but cannot reset a reached HARD.
27. Historical manual-close repair accepts only one uniquely matched, fully filled SELL from authenticated symbol/time-bounded order history with the exact outcome and quantity needed to flatten the recorded basis. Because Gemini order history omits fees, charge the published standard taker fee rounded up as a conservative upper bound. Any ambiguity, malformed row or full bounded result blocks activation.
28. A definitely-unsent maker leg invalidated by a same-symbol fill, accounting transition, cutoff, lifecycle block, exposure change, or fresh-book change cancels that symbol's remaining risk-adding entries and returns control to fill safety/ranking. It must not drain unrelated markets. Global permission loss, policy/scope mismatch and ambiguous send outcomes halt and drain. Stream loss/stale account data first remove entry permission; only invariant 32 can authorize same-process recovery. Supervisor recovery requires an atomic result bound to its exact session; missing or malformed results fail closed.
29. Deposits, withdrawals, liquidity rewards, and maker rebates are authenticated non-trading cash. They may reconcile expected cash only when exact IDs, amounts, status, currency, timestamps, and the net cash delta agree. They never write execution fills or realized P&L. Unknown, ambiguous, over-limit, malformed, or non-exact activity blocks new risk.
30. Local early exits reduce earning opportunities, not the venue denominator. Window estimates and reported counts stay distinct; missing evidence never confirms current/final qualification.
31. Typed transient periodic-read failures can hold entries/retry within the same permit while fresh classified account WS and safety/exit workers remain active. Never retry mutations, forgive missed uptime, clear another hold, reset loss/deadline state or bypass expiry. Malformed data, ambiguity and stale account state stay fail-closed.
32. Same-process transport recovery is limited to three reconnect attempts per affected stream and 60 seconds total. Pause entries, relearn server limits, resubscribe, discard old depth generations, and prove fresh WS/REST positions, exact expected cash, ownership and terminal order evidence before resuming. Read REST active orders before cancellation: cancel-on-disconnect is neither fill evidence nor permission for duplicate cancels. Missing fills/unknown mutations fail closed. Preserve original deadlines, losses, sticky pools and session expiry. Private recovery defers exits until account proof; public recovery allows exact exits only with fresh executable depth and healthy private state. No stale-price exit, deadline extension or execution replay.
33. Persist selected pool/event/symbol identity without transferring uptime between events or days. Rediscovery prefers the same safe identity. A v6 catalog session may retire an expired/inactive owner only after terminal cancellations, no unresolved intents/deadlines, and fresh authenticated flat proof. Inventory retains its owner. New symbols remain untradeable until synchronized depth, current eligibility and existing risk/ownership gates pass; discovery and subscriptions remain bounded and paced.
