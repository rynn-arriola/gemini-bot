# Control journal recovery and live acceptance

September 16, 2026. During the controlled deployment stop, the canonical SQLite control journal was found physically malformed: the file was truncated while its header still referenced pages beyond the file. The process was already stopped, Gemini showed one known position and zero active orders, and no restart was attempted against the damaged file. The corruption's root cause is not proven.

The original was preserved at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\GeminiRewardBot\control\production.corrupt-20260916-1108.sqlite`. Reconstruction began from the last verified backup and accepted recovered economic rows only after matching all 13 fills to authenticated per-symbol terminal order identity and executed quantity. Replayed fills matched the current single position. Stable REST and private WebSocket proof agreed on positions and stayed within the previously approved one-time cash incident boundary; that exception remains consumed and cannot be reused. One original deadline, sticky ZEC ownership, exact account snapshot and complete current risk classification were restored. The rebuilt database had zero nonterminal intents and passed `PRAGMA quick_check` before and after installation.

A separate full production `prepare-live` then completed without mutation: 19 tracked, 16 ranked, one reconciled position and zero active orders. Source `1fef292` and a fresh version-7 account/build/policy-bound permit with no uptime override activated through the persistent Scheduled Task. At live verification, the supervisor had zero restarts, runtime phase was `quoting`, and authenticated Gemini REST reported 22 active orders across 11 markets, one position and zero active orders on ZEC. ZEC reported `exit_depth_unavailable` through its independent exit worker while unrelated markets stayed quoted.

This validates the requested market isolation for known no-buyer inventory. It does not guarantee a buyer, reward qualification, host recovery or database self-repair. Unknown mutation outcomes, external orders, REST/WebSocket disagreement, missing ownership/deadline evidence and storage/integrity failures remain account-wide fail-closed conditions. Atomic independent journal backup/rotation and corruption-cause analysis remain follow-up work.
